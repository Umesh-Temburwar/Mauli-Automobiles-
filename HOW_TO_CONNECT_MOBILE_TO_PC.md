## Quick Summary
**PC par koi bhi software ya driver install karne ki zaroorat nahi hai!**  
Aapka Android mobile phone seedhe Windows PC ke sath standard wireless Bluetooth keyboard ki tarah pair ho jata hai.

---

### Step 1: Turn ON PC Bluetooth
1. Press `Win + I` to open **Settings**.
2. Click **Bluetooth & devices**.
3. Ensure **Bluetooth** is **ON**.
4. Click **Add device** > **Bluetooth**.

### Step 2: Open MAULI BARCODE App on Phone & Pair
1. Open **MAULI BARCODE** on your Android phone.
2. Grant Camera and Bluetooth permissions when prompted.
3. Tap on **Bluetooth Connect to Tally** or open the Devices tab.
4. Tap on your **PC Name** from the Bluetooth list.
5. Confirm the matching pairing code on both phone and PC.
6. Windows will say: *"Your Bluetooth keyboard is ready to go"*.

### Step 3: Open TallyPrime / Notepad / Excel & Scan!
1. Open **TallyPrime**, **Excel**, **Notepad**, or your billing software on Windows.
2. Click inside the item/barcode input field (blinking cursor).
3. Aim your phone camera at any barcode or click **Direct Send to Tally**.
4. The barcode number is typed instantly into your PC with an automatic `Enter` key!
