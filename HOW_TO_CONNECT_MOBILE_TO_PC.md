## Quick Summary
**PC par koi software open karne ki zaroorat nahi hai!** Mobile phone seedhe PC ke sath wireless keyboard ki tarah pair ho jata hai.

---

### Step 1: Turn ON PC Bluetooth
1. Press `Win + I` to open **Settings**.
2. Click **Bluetooth & devices**.
3. Ensure **Bluetooth** is **ON**.
4. Click **Add device** > **Bluetooth**.

### Step 2: Open App on Phone & Pair
1. Open **HID Barcode Scanner** on your Android phone.
2. Grant Camera and Bluetooth permissions when prompted.
3. Tap on your **PC Name** from the Bluetooth list.
4. Confirm the pairing code on both phone and PC.
5. Windows will say: *"Your Bluetooth keyboard is ready"*.

### Step 3: Open Any Software on PC & Scan!
1. Open **Notepad**, **Excel**, **TallyPrime**, or your billing software.
2. Click inside the text area (blinking cursor).
3. Aim your phone camera at a barcode.
4. The barcode types into your PC immediately!
